FCIDUMP files written by alps2fcidump, solved by other codes (2026-10-26, ALPS e79167a81)

The models are those of t9_fcidump.py plus a periodic Hubbard+V chain (src/tools/fcidump/tests/hubbard.par)
and a 12-site open Hubbard+V chain. Each is solved by ALPS fulldiag (sparsediag for L=12) in the same
(Nup, Ndown) sector; with FCIDUMP="file" set, that run wrote the same file as alps2fcidump in every case.
max|dE| is the largest difference from ALPS over the 4 lowest levels (ground state only for block2, and
for XDiag at L=12).

  PySCF 2.14.0    pyscf.fci.direct_spin1, reads the FCIDUMP itself
  block2 0.5.4    DMRG (SZ), reads the FCIDUMP itself; bond dimension up to 1000; open geometries only

  XDiag 0.6.0     Electron block (Julia); FCIDUMP read by a separate parser, terms built from Cdagup/Cup products

Jordan-Wigner qubit Hamiltonians from the same FCIDUMP files

The FCIDUMP is mapped to a qubit Hamiltonian (2 norb qubits) by Jordan-Wigner, and that Hamiltonian is
diagonalized in the (Nup, Ndown) sector. max|dE| is the largest difference from ALPS over the 4 lowest levels.
The two Hubbard rings are also compared with the Bethe ansatz (Lieb-Wu equations) ground-state energy.
